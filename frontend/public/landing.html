The Problem

Many people suffer in silence
rather than risk being judged.

In Sri Lanka, strong social stigma surrounds mental health struggles, sexual-health concerns, and addiction. The fear of being recognised at a clinic — or judged by someone who knows you — keeps people from seeking help.

Crisis helplines exist for emergencies. Therapy platforms exist for those who can book and pay. But there's nothing in between — no free, anonymous, multi-topic place to simply ask about the things you're most ashamed to discuss.

"I'd rather suffer in silence than risk someone I know finding out."

How It Works

Four gentle steps to being heard.

You're assigned a random session ID. No name, no email, no phone number — nothing that identifies you. The only thing we ask is a broad age band (like "19–25") so we can tailor our tone and route younger users to child-specific support. That's it.

Choose a topic — mental health, sexual health, addiction, or general health — or just start typing. The AI understands natural language and responds with care. It will never judge you, rush you, or tell you you're wrong for feeling what you feel.

The AI helps you understand your situation and options. It provides information, not diagnosis. It will never tell you that you "have" a condition or what medication to take. Think of it as a knowledgeable, kind first step — not a replacement for a professional.

If the AI detects a crisis, you'll see real helpline numbers immediately. If it recognises the situation needs a human, you can request an anonymous conversation with a verified doctor — your identity is never shared. If the doctor advises an in-person visit, you'll see their clinic details. The decision, and the contact, are entirely yours.

Safety & Privacy

Your identity is never
the price of getting help.

Verified, not assumed

Every doctor checked against SLMC

Zero identity fields

No name, NIC, email, or phone — anywhere in the app. Verified by design, not just policy.

Encrypted & minimal retention

All traffic uses HTTPS. Stored chats are encrypted. Mood logs are never linked to an identity. You can clear your data anytime.

Verified professionals only

Every doctor is checked against SLMC records before they can accept a chat. You see a verified badge — never an unverified stranger.

Information, not diagnosis

The AI helps you understand — it never tells you that you "have" a condition or what to take. Real medical decisions are always handed to a professional.

No third-party tracking

No analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels inside sensitive flows. Your conversation stays between you and the platform.
